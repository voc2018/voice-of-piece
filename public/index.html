Our vision

Heard as they are

Many of the painters we work with have painted quietly for years, with little recognition and limited materials. Their work deserves to be seen for what it is, not for how loudly it is explained.

We do not ask them to speak louder. We build the place where their work can be found, credited and fairly paid, so that talent is recognised for the talent it is.

They do not say much. They simply smile, and wait to hear what you see in their work.

“

About

Voice of Piece

Voice of Piece connects Tanzanian artists with people around the world who want to live with their work.

Each artist has a home of their own online, with their name, their story and their paintings. Their work reaches buyers through our shop, and their name, rights and fair payment travel with every piece.

For artists

Join Voice of Piece

Voice of Piece warmly welcomes new Tanzanian artists. If you are a painter who dreams of sharing your art with the world, we would love to hear from you.

To apply, send us photos of a few recent works, your name, and a short note about how and where you paint. We read every message and will write back.
