meet the artist

Ezekiel.

After finishing secondary school in 2020, I didn't go on to college, because I didn't like the course. So I decided to grow my talent for drawing through practice, and I spent the whole of 2021 practising.

At home, many believe that success means going to college, and they did not believe drawing could succeed or earn a living. No one in my community had been a painter before. I answer by working very hard, sharing my work in more places, and asking God, who gave me this talent, to grow it. One day I will succeed, and they will believe and learn.

  • Arusha
  • Drawing for 4 years
  • Pencil, blue pen and acrylic

About the work작품 이야기Kuhusu kazi

Translated from the artist's own answers in Kiswahili, lightly edited for clarity.

작가가 스와힐리어로 직접 쓴 답변을 옮기고 읽기 쉽게 다듬었습니다.

Kwa maneno ya msanii mwenyewe, yamehaririwa kidogo ili yasomeke vizuri.

How it began시작Nilivyoanza

After finishing secondary school in 2020, I didn’t continue to college because I didn’t like the course. Instead I decided to grow my gift for drawing, and I devoted the whole of 2021 to practice.

2020년에 중등학교를 마친 뒤, 전공이 맞지 않아 대학에 진학하지 않았습니다. 대신 그림에 대한 재능을 키우기로 마음먹고 2021년 한 해를 온전히 연습에 쏟았습니다.

Baada ya kumaliza kidato cha nne mwaka 2020, sikuendelea na chuo kwa sababu sikuipenda kozi ile. Hivyo niliamua kukuza kipaji changu cha uchoraji kwa kufanya mazoezi, na nilijikita kwenye mazoezi mwaka mzima wa 2021.

Style and themes화풍과 주제Mtindo na maudhui

I work in several media: black-and-white pencil, blue ballpoint pen and acrylic. The messages I return to most are the love between a mother and her child, new hope, and victory.

흑백 연필화, 파란 볼펜화, 아크릴화 등 여러 방식으로 작업합니다. 가장 자주 담는 메시지는 엄마와 아이 사이의 사랑, 새로운 희망, 그리고 승리입니다.

Ninafanya aina mbalimbali za sanaa, ikiwemo michoro ya penseli ya rangi nyeusi na nyeupe, michoro ya kalamu ya bluu, na uchoraji wa rangi za akriliki. Ujumbe ninaoupenda mara nyingi ni upendo kati ya mama na mtoto, tumaini jipya na ushindi.

Why I draw그리는 이유Kwa nini ninachora

I have loved drawing since I was little, when I drew cartoons. What drew me in most was seeing other artists’ work on social media, including YouTube. It won me over and gave me the drive to become an artist.

어릴 때 만화를 그리던 시절부터 그림을 좋아했습니다. 무엇보다 유튜브 같은 소셜 미디어에서 다른 작가들의 작품을 보며 마음이 끌렸고, 화가가 되고 싶다는 의지가 더 커졌습니다.

Nimependa sanaa ya uchoraji tangu nikiwa mdogo, nilipokuwa nikipenda kuchora katuni. Lakini kilichonivutia zaidi ni kuona kazi za wengine kwenye mitandao ya kijamii, ikiwemo YouTube. Nilishawishika, na ikaniongezea hamasa ya kuwa msanii.

What moves me영감Kinachonihamasisha

Family, first of all. When a family lives well, it gives you strength, and as an artist you find many messages in it. Many of my pictures of a mother’s love come from my own mother.

무엇보다 가족입니다. 가족이 잘 지낼 때 힘을 얻고, 화가로서 많은 메시지를 발견하게 됩니다. 엄마의 사랑을 그린 제 그림들은 대부분 저의 어머니에게서 나왔습니다.

Cha kwanza kabisa ni familia. Familia inapoishi vizuri inakupa nguvu, na kama msanii unapata jumbe mbalimbali. Picha zangu nyingi za upendo wa mama kwa mtoto zinatokana na mama yangu mzazi.

The road so far걸어온 길Safari yangu

At home, many believe success means going to college, and no one thought drawing could lead anywhere or earn a living. No one in my community had ever been a painter. I answer by working very hard, sharing my work in more places, and asking God, who gave me this gift, to help it grow. One day I will succeed, and they will believe and learn.

주변에서는 대학에 가야 성공한다고 믿었고, 그림으로 성공하거나 생계를 꾸릴 수 있다고 생각하지 않았습니다. 우리 마을에는 화가가 한 명도 없었으니까요. 저는 누구보다 열심히 작업하고, 더 많은 곳에 작품을 알리고, 이 재능을 주신 하나님께 그것을 키워 달라고 기도하며 답하고 있습니다. 언젠가 저는 해낼 것이고, 그들도 믿고 배우게 될 것입니다.

Kikwazo kikubwa ni kwamba jamii ya nyumbani inaamini kufanikiwa ni kwenda chuoni, na haiamini kwamba uchoraji unaweza kuleta mafanikio au kipato. Katika jamii yangu hakujawahi kuwa na mchoraji kabla yangu. Ninakabiliana na hili kwa kufanya kazi kwa bidii sana, kusambaza kazi zangu sehemu nyingi zaidi, na kumwomba Mungu aliyenipa kipaji hiki akikuze. Siku moja nitafanikiwa, nao wataamini na kujifunza.

A work I love아끼는 작품Kazi ninayoipenda

Twiga na Kipepeo (Giraffe and Butterfly), which carries the message of the scent of victory. It became one of my most loved pictures, and it opened the way to work with people who went on to support my art.

‘기린과 나비(Twiga na Kipepeo)’입니다. 승리의 향기라는 메시지를 담았습니다. 많은 사랑을 받은 그림이고, 이 작품 덕분에 제 그림을 후원해 주는 분들과 함께 일할 기회를 얻었습니다.

Moja ya kazi ninazozipenda ni Twiga na Kipepeo, inayobeba ujumbe wa harufu ya ushindi. Picha hii ilikuwa moja ya picha zilizopendwa sana, na ilinipa nafasi ya kufanya kazi na watu walioisaidia sanaa yangu.

Looking ahead앞으로의 꿈Malengo yangu

To teach young people the art of drawing, and to form a group of artists who work together.

어린 친구들에게 그림을 가르치고, 함께 작업하는 화가 모임을 만드는 것입니다.

Malengo yangu ya muda mrefu ni kujitahidi kuwafundisha vijana sanaa ya uchoraji, na kuunda kikundi cha wachoraji tunaofanya kazi pamoja.
