Artist · Voice of Piece

Simon Venance Mussa

Charcoal and pastel, built stroke by stroke until a face, a hide, a glance becomes almost real.

  • Tanzania
  • Drawing since age 10
  • Charcoal & pastel on canvas

In the artist's words작가의 말Kwa maneno ya msanii

About the work작품 이야기Kuhusu kazi

Translated from the artist's own answers in Kiswahili, lightly edited for clarity.

작가가 스와힐리어로 직접 쓴 답변을 옮기고 읽기 쉽게 다듬었습니다.

Kwa maneno ya msanii mwenyewe, yamehaririwa kidogo ili yasomeke vizuri.

How it began시작Nilivyoanza

I was born in 2001 and started drawing at ten. I entered the art market in 2015 and have worked with many different clients and companies. I love drawing because a drawn picture carries a complete reality, straight from the artist’s hand.

2001년에 태어나 열 살에 그림을 시작했고, 2015년부터 작품을 팔기 시작해 여러 개인과 기업과 일해 왔습니다. 손으로 그린 그림에는 작가에게서 곧바로 나온 온전한 현실이 담겨 있기에 그림을 사랑합니다.

Nimezaliwa mwaka 2001, nimeanza kuchora nikiwa na miaka kumi, na nimeingia kwenye soko la sanaa mwaka 2015. Nimefanya kazi na watu na makampuni mbalimbali. Ninapenda kuchora kwa sababu picha za kuchorwa zina uhalisia kamili unaotoka moja kwa moja kwa msanii.

What I draw그리는 것Ninachochora

Hyper-realistic pictures in charcoal pencil and pastel on canvas. My subjects are the realities of our society and our environment.

캔버스 위에 목탄 연필과 파스텔로 극사실적인 그림을 그립니다. 주로 우리 사회와 환경의 현실을 다룹니다.

Ninachora kwa penseli ya mkaa na pastel kwenye turubai, picha za uhalisia uliopitiliza (hyper-realistic). Mada kuu katika sanaa yangu ni uhalisia wa mambo katika jamii na mazingira.

Why I draw그리는 이유Kwa nini ninachora

To educate, and to carry the right message to the community around me. I draw because I was born with this gift, because I want to share ideas with society, and because I feel at peace when I draw.

사람들을 일깨우고, 주변 공동체에 올바른 메시지를 전하고 싶습니다. 타고난 재능이 있기 때문에, 사회와 생각을 나누고 싶기 때문에, 그리고 그릴 때 마음이 평화롭기 때문에 그립니다.

Kuelimisha na kufikisha ujumbe sahihi kwa jamii inayonizunguka. Ninachora kwa sababu nimezaliwa na kipaji cha kuchora, kwa sababu napenda kufikisha mawazo tofauti katika jamii, na kwa sababu ninajisikia amani ninapochora.

How I work작업 방식Jinsi ninavyofanya kazi

An idea comes from my surroundings. I research it and test whether it is right, then choose the materials that will carry it into a drawing. I use a reference photograph for true realism, then draw — adding art into the idea until the picture is complete.

주변 환경에서 아이디어를 얻으면 조사하고 그 생각이 옳은지 따져 봅니다. 그다음 그것을 그림으로 옮길 재료를 고르고, 사실감을 위해 참고 사진을 보며 그립니다. 그림이 완성될 때까지 아이디어에 예술을 더해 갑니다.

Ninapata wazo kutokana na mazingira yanayonizunguka, kisha ninalifanyia utafiti na kutathmini kama ni sahihi. Ninachagua vifaa nitakavyotumia kulihamishia wazo hilo kwenye mchoro. Ninatumia pia picha ya marejeo ili kupata uhalisia halisi, kisha ninaanza kuchora, nikiongeza sanaa ndani ya wazo langu mpaka picha ikamilike.

Materials재료Vifaa

Woodless charcoal pencils, charcoal blocks, pencil and electric erasers, paper stumps, a fine eraser pen, dry brushes, cotton, canvas, pastels, a ruler and fixative.

심만 있는 목탄 연필, 목탄 블록, 연필 지우개와 전동 지우개, 찰필, 세밀 지우개, 마른 붓, 솜, 캔버스, 파스텔, 자, 정착액을 씁니다.

Penseli za mkaa zisizo na mbao, vipande vya mkaa, kifutio cha penseli na kifutio cha umeme, vitambaa vya kuchanganya (paper stumps), kifutio kidogo, brashi kavu, pamba, turubai, pastel, rula na fixative.

A work I love아끼는 작품Kazi ninayoipenda

Blindfold — a woman with a red cloth tied over her eyes and ears, weeping, her mouth closed while her shadow’s mouth is open. It says that in our societies, especially in Africa, women endure hard things under customs that limit their freedom, yet have nowhere to speak of it. They hurt inside but do not show it.

〈Blindfold〉입니다. 빨간 천으로 눈과 귀를 가린 채 우는 여인을 그렸는데, 여인의 입은 닫혀 있지만 그림자의 입은 열려 있습니다. 우리 사회, 특히 아프리카에서 여성들이 자유를 억누르는 관습 속에 힘든 일을 겪으면서도 호소할 곳이 없다는 이야기입니다. 안으로는 아프지만 밖으로는 드러내지 못하는 것이죠.

Blindfold. Ni picha ya mwanamke aliyefungwa kitambaa chekundu machoni na masikioni akilia machozi, mdomo wake ukiwa umefumba lakini kivuli chake kina mdomo wazi. Picha hii inatupa ujumbe kwamba katika jamii, hasa za Kiafrika, wanawake wanapitia mambo magumu kutokana na mila na desturi zinazokandamiza uhuru wao, lakini hawana mahali pa kulalamika. Wanaumia ndani lakini nje hawaonyeshi.

Looking ahead앞으로의 꿈Malengo yangu

To become a greater artist by working ever more creatively, and to lift up young artists.

더 창의적으로 작업해 더 큰 화가가 되고, 젊은 화가들을 이끌어 주는 것입니다.

Malengo yangu ni kuwa msanii mkubwa zaidi kwa kufanya kazi yangu kwa ubunifu zaidi, na kuwainua wasanii wachanga.
