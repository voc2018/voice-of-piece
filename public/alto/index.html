About the artist

Drawings that show people and forms with striking realism, made in charcoal and graphite to speak about love and joy.

To show my feelings through drawing. I believe society needs a message from me through this art, so that it can learn.

Emotional experiences I have lived through, and the nature and culture of our country. To take part in major exhibitions abroad, and to grow my art by learning from others and exchanging ideas.

How it began시작Nilivyoanza

Alto began drawing professionally in 2017. He has exhibited at home and abroad, including the East Africa Art Biennale 2019 at Nafasi Art Space and the Art and Human Rights exhibition at Concordia University, Montreal, in May 2023.

Alto는 2017년부터 전문 작가로서 본격적으로 그림을 그리기 시작했습니다. 2019년 Nafasi Art Space에서 열린 동아프리카 아트 비엔날레, 2023년 5월 캐나다 몬트리올 콘코디아 대학교의 '예술과 인권' 전시 등 국내외 전시에 참여했습니다.

Alto Augustino Haule alianza rasmi kuchora kama mchoraji mbobezi mwaka 2017. Ameshiriki maonyesho ndani na nje ya nchi, ikiwemo East Africa Art Biennale 2019 katika Nafasi Art Space, na Art and Human Rights Exhibition katika Chuo Kikuu cha Concordia, Montreal, mwezi Mei 2023.

Style and themes화풍과 주제Mtindo na maudhui

His drawings render people and objects with striking realism. Working mostly in charcoal pencil and graphite, he pours his feelings into each drawing, and his work speaks again and again for love and joy.

그의 그림은 사람과 사물의 형태를 놀라울 만큼 사실적으로 담아냅니다. 주로 목탄 연필과 흑연으로 감정을 표현하며, 작품 속에서 늘 사랑과 기쁨을 이야기합니다.

Sanaa ya Alto Haule inaonyesha kwa kiwango cha juu uhalisia wa maumbo ya vitu na watu. Mara nyingi hutumia penseli ya mkaa na grafaiti kuonyesha hisia zake kupitia michoro, na kazi zake huzungumzia na kuhimiza upendo na furaha.

Why I draw그리는 이유Kwa nini ninachora

To pour my feelings into drawings through this gift. I believe society needs a message from me through this art, so that people can learn from it.

재능을 통해 제 감정을 그림에 담고 싶었습니다. 사람들이 배울 수 있도록, 이 그림을 통해 사회에 전해야 할 메시지가 제게 있다고 믿습니다.

Kuonyesha hisia zangu kwenye michoro kupitia kipaji hiki. Ninaamini jamii inahitaji ujumbe kutoka kwangu kupitia sanaa hii ya uchoraji ili ipate kujifunza.

How I work작업 방식Jinsi ninavyofanya kazi

An idea takes hold of me, and when it keeps returning to my mind it pushes me to work on it. I draw with charcoal pencil and graphite on paper or canvas. First I lay down the outer form, then I build the shading inside it until the subject feels real.

어떤 생각이 목마르게 떠오르고, 그 생각이 머릿속에서 계속 되풀이되면 작업을 시작하게 됩니다. 목탄 연필과 흑연으로 종이나 캔버스에 그리는데, 먼저 바깥 윤곽을 잡은 뒤 그 안에 그림자를 쌓아 대상이 실제처럼 느껴지게 만듭니다.

Huwa ninapata kiu ya wazo fulani, na likiendelea kujirudia kichwani mwangu linanihimiza kulifanyia kazi. Ninachora kwa penseli ya mkaa na grafaiti kwenye karatasi au turubai. Kwanza ninaandaa umbo la nje la mchoro, kisha ninatengeneza vivuli ndani ya umbo hilo ili kuonyesha uhalisia zaidi wa kitu husika.

What moves me영감Kinachonihamasisha

Emotional moments I have lived through, and the nature and culture of our country.

제가 겪어 온 감정의 순간들, 그리고 우리나라의 자연과 문화입니다.

Matukio ya kihisia niliyoyapitia maishani mwangu, pamoja na uasilia na utamaduni wa nchi yetu.

A work I love아끼는 작품Kazi ninayoipenda

A drawing of a crying child. I made it because I have met many families living in hard conditions, and so often it is the children who suffer most, going without what they need to grow. I drew it with sadness in my heart, in charcoal pencil and graphite on paper.

우는 아이를 그린 작품입니다. 어려운 환경에서 살아가는 가정을 많이 만났는데, 가장 큰 고통을 겪는 것은 자라는 데 꼭 필요한 것을 누리지 못하는 아이들이었습니다. 슬픈 마음을 안고 종이 위에 목탄 연필과 흑연으로 그렸습니다.

Mchoro wa mtoto anayelia. Niliufanya kwa sababu nimekutana na familia nyingi zinazoishi katika mazingira magumu, na mara nyingi wanaoumia zaidi ni watoto kwa kukosa mahitaji yao muhimu katika malezi. Niliuchora nikiwa na huzuni moyoni, kwa penseli ya mkaa na grafaiti kwenye karatasi.

Looking ahead앞으로의 꿈Malengo yangu

To show my work in major exhibitions abroad, and to keep growing by learning from others and exchanging ideas.

해외의 큰 전시에 참여하는 것, 그리고 다른 작가들과 생각을 나누며 배우면서 제 그림을 키워 가는 것입니다.

Kufikia hatua ya kushiriki maonyesho makubwa nje ya nchi, na kukuza sanaa yangu kwa kujifunza kutoka kwa wengine na kubadilishana mawazo.
